Privacy policy

In short: no advertising, no trackers, no sharing for business purposes.

1. Controller

The controller for this private project is the GitHub user {{operator}} (contact via GitHub, see the legal notice). This is a private, non-commercial offering.

2. Use without an account (local mode)

In local mode there are no accounts and no login. The dashboard stores settings and server data on the computer it runs on. No access logs with IP addresses are kept and no data is sent to the operator. An optional access password is stored only as a secure hash (scrypt).

3. Use with an account (multi-user mode)

If the operator has switched on multi-user mode, the following data is processed:

  • Account: email address, an optional name, role, time of last login, and the password only as a hash (never in plain text). When you sign in with Google, the dashboard receives your name and verified email address from Google.
  • Server data: your Minecraft servers with worlds, settings, player lists, backups and files, as well as the assigned ports and quotas.
  • Session: a random session token (stored only as a hash, valid for 14 days).
  • IP address: kept only briefly in memory to limit repeated failed login attempts. It is not stored permanently.

Purpose and legal basis: providing the service and protecting against abuse (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). Retention: until your account is deleted by you or the operator. Sessions expire after 14 days of inactivity.

4. Cookies and local storage in your browser

  • Strictly necessary cookies for login (sid or dash_access) and, only during Google sign-in, short-lived cookies that protect against forgery (oauth_state, oauth_pkce, 10 minutes).
  • Local storage (localStorage): display settings such as appearance and language, and “don’t ask again” checkmarks. This data stays in your browser.
  • There are no advertising or analytics cookies and no trackers.

5. Third-party services

For its features the dashboard accesses the following services. Only the data needed for the respective feature is transmitted:

  • Modrinth (searching and downloading mods, plugins, modpacks; partly directly from your browser, so Modrinth learns your IP address).
  • Mojang / Microsoft (Minecraft versions, player profiles for the whitelist) and PaperMC, Purpur, FabricMC, NeoForged (server downloads).
  • GeyserMC (Bedrock support, resolving Xbox gamertags).
  • GitHub (checking for new dashboard versions and downloading updates).
  • Let’s Encrypt (free HTTPS certificate, if the operator sets it up; domain and email address are transmitted).
  • Google (only if you sign in with it).

The privacy policies of these providers apply.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Contact the operator for this (see above). Your account and server data can be deleted completely by the operator.

7. Security

Passwords are stored only as hashes, and so are session tokens. The operator should make the dashboard available over HTTPS only. Still, no transmission or storage is absolutely secure. Use a long password that you use nowhere else.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when the dashboard changes. The version published here applies.
